Benefits of partnership.

Revenue without operational drag. Reputation protection without a service department. A concrete answer the next time a client calls about an eighteen-month-old stain.

Up to 35% commission

Approved partners earn commission on every plan they specify, whether the client purchases at closeout or later through the plans page. Commission rates and payment timing are detailed in your Partner Agreement.

Your first plan, on us — up to $5,000 of coverage

Your first specified deal earns a boosted 40% commission — the 35% standard rate plus a 5% promo boost, capped at $5,000 of coverage value. Claim it on your dashboard and tell us the client.

Your client calls one number. We handle the claim, the repair, and the resolution. You stay focused on the design work.

Need professional hands for delivery, unboxing, room placement, or installation? Partners can request white-glove service directly through their account. Pricing is negotiated per project.
